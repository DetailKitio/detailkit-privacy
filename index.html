DetailKit Privacy Policy

Effective: January 1, 2025

Your Data Stays on Your Device

DetailKit stores all of your business data — jobs, clients, inventory, profits, and settings — locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate servers, and your data is never uploaded to or stored on our infrastructure.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud Drive enabled, Apple may sync your app data across your own Apple devices through your personal iCloud account. DetailKit has no access to or visibility into that data.

Camera Access

DetailKit requests camera access for scanning product barcodes. Camera images are processed on-device. We do not store or transmit photos to any external service.

Notifications

If you enable job reminders or low-stock alerts, DetailKit will request permission to send local notifications. These are generated entirely on your device and do not involve external servers.

No Third-Party Sharing

DetailKit does not share, sell, or rent your information to any third parties. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Data Security

Your data is protected by iOS device encryption and the secure Keychain for sensitive values.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete all app data by removing DetailKit from your device.
